Step by Step: How We Made This Podcast

Production Workflow & Tools

Here is the step-by-step process we followed to create our episode:

  1. Topic Research & Selecting the Top 5 Questions: We searched for information about the topic we were assigned, and from this information, we chose the 5 best questions related to it.
  2. Scriptwriting & Outline: Using those 5 key questions, we wrote a clear script including the introduction, answers, and closing remarks.
  3. Technical Setup: We chose a quiet room to avoid echoes.
  4. Recording the Episode: We recorded our voices on separate tracks using RECstudio y Spreaker.
  5. Editing & Post-Production: We cut out long silences, removed mistakes, added intro and outro music, and intrducing sound effects.
